about=SemanticGIS organises Danish and European geospatial data as a network of spheres, twigs and leaves: each leaf is a question you can ask of the data (for example "Which nature protection designations apply here?"). This plugin lets you browse that network or search it by question, and adds the services behind each leaf as layers. Every layer is tagged with the leaf and dataset it came from. The catalogue is published at semanticgis.dk and checked against the live services.
